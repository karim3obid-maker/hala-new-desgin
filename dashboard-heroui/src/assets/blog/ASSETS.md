# Hala blog imagery
Created 2026-10-23 using the built-in ImageGen tool, with the original `src/assets/hala-logo.png` as identity reference. These are photorealistic AI illustrations, not documentary photographs of Hala staff, customers or facilities. The UI identifies them as illustrative.

- `blog-cod.webp`: customer order confirmation at a Gulf ecommerce workstation.
- `blog-fulfillment.webp`: product scanning and parcel preparation.
- `blog-gulf.webp`: planning Gulf market expansion with a printed map.

Optimized locally to1200×800 WebP. Full generation prompts are preserved in `GENERATION.json`. Generated preview alternatives and approved map proposal are preserved separately under `design/proposals/`, with their own generation manifest.
